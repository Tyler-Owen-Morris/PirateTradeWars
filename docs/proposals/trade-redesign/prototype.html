Before: the current trade screen

Captured from the live client (dev/2026-10-23-batch) with Playwright. Full-size PNGs are in the repo at docs/proposals/trade-redesign/before-*.png; the critique is in PROPOSAL.md.

  1. Buy Max is off-screen. At 1280×720 the Buy Max button sits at y = 912; at 375×667 at y = 980. The detail panel is appended under the list and the whole dialog scrolls.
  2. Selection leaks across tabs. Select Cotton on Buy, switch to Sell: the Sale Details panel offers to sell Cotton (38 g) you do not own, while the one good you do own is shown unselected.
  3. Trend colours lie. On the Buy tab a cheap good (Sugar ↓47%) is red and a dear one green: backwards for a buyer. (The "trend" is really the difference from the base price.)
  4. Generic web-app look. Slate dialog, orange header, shadcn tabs, lucide icons: nothing in-world. "Select" buttons are the only thing in each row you can press.
  5. Max fires immediately with no preview; the selection is cleared before the server answers; a stale red alert lingers; unrelated socket errors appear in the trade screen.
