## Spec questions / clarifications (for the producer to append to WORLD_SPEC.md)
1. **The tiles are gzipped.** The raw set is 19.8 MiB, over the 15 MB budget.
   - Files are `t_<tx>_<ty>.bin.gz`, and the manifest has `"encoding":"gzip"`.
   - Decompressed, each one is the spec's 65536-byte Int8 tile.
   - Serve them as opaque bytes, with no `Content-Encoding`.
   - **W1 (server loader) and W3 (client) must handle this.**
2. **What "dock ≤ 400 from the coast near the town" means.** I read it as: the dock's sdf is in (80, 400], and the dock is near the town (town→dock ≤ 800; actual max 489).
3. **Some towns moved.** "Town = on land, near the coast" can't hold at the real coordinates for river ports at 4.9 km pixels. Seville, London and Bristol were snapped 284–444 units toward the estuary. The test caps the snap at 500.
4. **Lane endpoints are the docks.** Harbour approaches within 2500 units of a dock need only clearance ≥ 80 (100 everywhere else). Without that, several docks can't reach open water. The minimum clearance along all lanes is 72.
5. **There are 27 ports, not ~25.** I added San Juan, Willemstad, Saint-Pierre, Saint-Malo, Elmina, Île Sainte-Marie, Manila and Acapulco to the required list.
   - Nassau, Tortuga and Île Sainte-Marie belong to `pirates`.
   - Acapulco–Manila is an extra named lane.
6. **New file `shared/world/versions.json`** (`{land, ports, lanes}` content hashes), added so W4 can use `ports` as `portsVersion`.

## Notes for consumers
- **Lanes:**
  - Each lane is stored once per unordered pair, and `from` is the id that sorts first. Traverse it in either direction.
  - x is unwrapped, so it can go outside [0, C). Wrap it before use.
  - No leg is longer than 4000 metric units.
- **The map PNGs** are equirectangular with lon −180 at the left and lat +90 at the top: u = x/C, v = z/H.
