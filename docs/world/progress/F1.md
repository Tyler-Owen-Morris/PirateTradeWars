### B2 in detail
1. **Inaccurate fire gate.** ±12° of the beam ± aimError noise, judged on the pose before the tick's rudder (the World fires after movement; one tick of rudder = 0.15 rad). 1 hit in 28 volleys on a still target. Now `fireTolerance` (hit circle × 0.8 + half the fan), a one-tick lookahead with the real `stepShip` choosing the rudder (`NpcSystem.aim`), arc lead for turning targets (`turnRate` from the target's held rudder), fire anywhere up to 1.5 × engage range, aim error = per-volley slack.
2. **The 375↔1060 loop was a flee, not a fight.** A pirate evading an orbiting attacker's predicted position circles beside it (≈ 2 laps in 17 s). Q1's pirate was very likely fleeing a navy outside its 2,500 filter. Flee now commits to a course straight away from the threat, re-aimed only past 90° off astern.
3. **Fair play slot race.** A provoked pirate could lose both attacker slots to neighbours and give up. Self-defence for 60 s after a player's hit ignores the shield and the cap.
4. **Friendly fire** from a partner turned pirates on each other: ignored now; allies are never targets.
5. **Disengage.** hp ≤ (1 − courage) × 0.7 (pirate 35 %, privateer 28 %, navy 7 %) adds a decisive +3 flee (it lost to the fight score vs weaker ships before). Merchant surrender score raised to 10+ to stay above it.
6. Approach: the orbit controller now spirals in 30° off the line of sight (`APPROACH_MAX_K`) instead of running straight at the target. Turning radius is 20 × speed (80–180), inside the 435–510 band, so slowing to turn is unnecessary. Guns are starboard-only for players and NPCs (`spawnBroadside`); kept.

Sim numbers (30 s, seeds 1–3, pirate provoked by one hit): sloop vs still sloop 14–15 shots / 4–7 hits; brigantine vs still 17–18 / 15–17; brigantine vs straight sloop 12–13 / 11–13; vs circling sloop 14–18 / 7–14. Before: ~1 hit per 28 shots.

**Known limit:** a starboard-only sloop can't catch an equal-speed sloop running straight away (stern chase never closes); it gets its broadside in on a crossing/head-on pass (tested). Faster ships run slower ones down.

**Balance note for the producer:** pirates are now lethal. A pirate sloop sinks a still player sloop in ~30–40 s; a pirate brigantine sank a straight-sailing player sloop in 24 s. Soften via `aimError` or `HIT_TOLERANCE_FRAC` if needed.

### B1 note (spec vs numbers)
The clarification's formula clamps the *speed* to maxSpeed·sqrt(1 − hn²) (0.71·max at 45°). Because the heading still points into the coast, progress *along* the coast is speed × sqrt(1 − hn²) = 0.5·max at 45° (QA measured ~0.08·max before). If "≥ 0.6 of max along the coast" is the real target, the clamp would have to leave |v| unreduced (a spec change); I implemented the clarification literally and tested |v| ≥ 0.6·max plus along-coast ≥ 0.45·max.
